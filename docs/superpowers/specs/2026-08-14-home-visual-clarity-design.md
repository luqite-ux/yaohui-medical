# Home Visual Clarity Design

## Goal

Improve homepage readability and product presentation without changing the approved bright Yaohui Medical visual identity.

## Banner

- Give the left copy, actions, and statistics a 94% opaque white foundation.
- Fade that white foundation softly into the right-side imagery instead of using a hard divider.
- Preserve the right factory/product collage, scan line, and floating frame motion.
- Keep grid lines very faint and prevent brochure text from showing behind copy.
- On mobile, use a complete white foundation behind the copy.

## Product Photography

- Replace the three inconsistent source photos with coherent, high-resolution medical catalog photography.
- Use a bright white or very light gray studio background, soft natural shadows, consistent camera angle, and consistent product scale.
- Plaster of Paris Bandage: white plaster-coated gauze roll with a short unrolled section.
- Orthopedic Padding: soft white padding roll with visible fiber texture and a short unrolled section.
- Elastic Bandage: clean white elastic bandage rolls with one short unrolled section.
- Do not add text, logos, hands, medical claims, packaging, or unrelated props.

## Verification

- Automated source rule confirms the Banner readability layer and three new asset paths.
- Production build succeeds.
- Desktop and 390px mobile screenshots show readable Banner copy, consistent product cards, no clipping, and no console errors.
